---
name: mobile-ux-review
description: Checklist to review any screen before reporting done.
---
For each screen verify: one primary action; touch targets >= 48px; nothing clipped or overlapping at 360/390/430 px; safe areas respected; text >= 14px; contrast OK; loading/empty/error states present; no dead buttons (every button does something real or is labeled Demo); no more than 2 lines of body text per card.
Produce screenshots and a pass/fail list.
