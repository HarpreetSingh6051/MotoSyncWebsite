- Background true black #000000. Surface #070A10, card #0E1422.
- Accents: electric blue #10B1FB (primary), neon violet #8B5CF6 (secondary), success #10B981, alert red #EF4444 (alerts only). Amber/gold only for Golden Helmet PRO badge.
- Fonts: Space Grotesk for headings/numbers/telemetry, Inter for body.
- Radii 24 card, 18 button, 999 pill. Glass cards: blur + 1px white 8-14% border, glow on active.
- Voice: short, rider-friendly. Headline max 6 words, helper text max 12 words.
- Tagline: "Pack Connected. Ride in Sync."
- Forbidden in public/rider UI: admin, ERP, enterprise wording.
